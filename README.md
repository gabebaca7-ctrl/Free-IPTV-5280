# Free IPTV — USA (English)

A free, legal-source IPTV playlist built from the [iptv-org](https://iptv-org.github.io/iptv/) US collection. It **mirrors the paid StreamKing lineup**: channels that exist in both — with a stream that's actually alive — are listed first, in the paid list's order and groups, under the same names (Denver locals, ESPN, ESPN 2, A&E, AMC Thrillers, Golf Channel, Crunchyroll and more). All other free channels follow in their own groups. It complements the paid service; it doesn't replace it — some premium names (HBO, Starz, NFL Network…) only exist in the free world as pirate relays that are dead or unreliable, so they're not carried here. The real ones stay on the paid service.

**1,086 channels** — locals & national network feeds, entertainment, sports, movies, news, kids, comedy, classics, documentaries and music. English only, no religious channels, no Spanish-language channels. Every stream in the paid-mirror section was liveness-tested; dead relays were removed.

**Logos:** included for nearly every channel.

**Program guide (EPG):** built in — one merged guide file covering **346 channels** (Pluto TV, Samsung TV Plus and Plex networks), refreshed daily. The playlist header points your player at it automatically; if your player asks for the guide separately, use:
- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gabebaca7-ctrl/Free-IPTV-5280/main/epg.xml
