# Free IPTV — USA (English)

A free, legal-source IPTV playlist built from the [iptv-org](https://iptv-org.github.io/iptv/) US collection. Channels that also exist in the paid StreamKing lineup keep their paid names (Denver locals, ESPN, ESPN 2, TCM, Golf Channel, Crunchyroll and more), and the whole list is organized in a **Comcast/Xfinity-style category layout**: Denver Locals first (in broadcast order), then a **US Network Affiliates** section — 139 local ABC/CBS/CW/Fox/NBC/PBS/MyNet stations from other markets, ordered by network then callsign — then News, Sports, Movies, Entertainment, Kids & Family, Lifestyle, Documentary & Learning, and Music. It complements the paid service; it doesn't replace it — some premium names (HBO, Starz, NFL Network…) only exist in the free world as pirate relays that are dead or unreliable, so they're not carried here. The real ones stay on the paid service.

**533 channels** — locals, network affiliates, entertainment, sports, movies, kids, lifestyle, documentaries and music. English only. No religious channels, no Spanish-language channels, no duplicates. Standalone news networks are **Colorado only** (CBS News Colorado and the Denver locals) — national news/weather networks are not carried; other cities' stations live in the affiliates section as full station feeds. Dead pirate relays were removed.

**Logos:** included for nearly every channel.

**Program guide (EPG):** every channel in this playlist has guide data — that's a rule, not a coincidence. Channels were matched against six free guides (Pluto TV, Samsung TV Plus, Plex, and the epgshare US / US locals / US sports guides) with several matching passes; any channel with no guide anywhere was removed. The merged guide covers **all 533 channels** (about 14,300 programs, roughly the next 24 hours) and refreshes daily. The playlist header points your player at it automatically; if your player asks for the guide separately, use:
- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gabebaca7-ctrl/Free-IPTV-5280/main/epg.xml

Four paid-mirror channels were dropped by the EPG rule because no free guide exists for them anywhere: A&E, Beach TV Panama City, Fite 24/7 and Rally TV. They remain on the paid service.

One station, one guide slot: KPVM-LD (Pahrump, NV) is carried as **Movies! KPVM-LD** — its ACE Country Radio stream was swapped out, since both streams shared the station's single guide listing.
