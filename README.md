# Free IPTV — USA (English)

A free, legal-source IPTV playlist built from the [iptv-org](https://iptv-org.github.io/iptv/) US collection. It **mirrors the paid StreamKing lineup**: channels that exist in both — with a stream that's actually alive — are listed first, in the paid list's order and groups, under the same names (Denver locals, ESPN, ESPN 2, TCM, Golf Channel, Crunchyroll and more). All other free channels follow in their own groups. It complements the paid service; it doesn't replace it — some premium names (HBO, Starz, NFL Network…) only exist in the free world as pirate relays that are dead or unreliable, so they're not carried here. The real ones stay on the paid service.

**571 channels** — locals & national network feeds, entertainment, sports, movies, news, kids, comedy, classics, documentaries and music. English only. No religious channels, no Spanish-language channels, no duplicates. News is Denver/Colorado plus the big national networks only — no other cities' local news. Every stream in the paid-mirror section was liveness-tested; dead relays were removed.

**Logos:** included for nearly every channel.

**Program guide (EPG):** every channel in this playlist has guide data — that's a rule, not a coincidence. Channels were matched against six free guides (Pluto TV, Samsung TV Plus, Plex, and the epgshare US / US locals / US sports guides) with several matching passes; any channel with no guide anywhere was removed. The merged guide covers **all 571 channels** (about 25,000 programs, roughly the next 48 hours) and refreshes daily. The playlist header points your player at it automatically; if your player asks for the guide separately, use:
- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gabebaca7-ctrl/Free-IPTV-5280/main/epg.xml

Four paid-mirror channels were dropped by the EPG rule because no free guide exists for them anywhere: A&E, Beach TV Panama City, Fite 24/7 and Rally TV. They remain on the paid service.
