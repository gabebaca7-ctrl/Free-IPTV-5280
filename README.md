# Free IPTV — USA (English)

A free, legal-source IPTV playlist built from the [iptv-org](https://iptv-org.github.io/iptv/) US collection. Channels that also exist in the paid StreamKing lineup keep their paid names (Denver locals, ESPN, ESPN 2, TCM, Crunchyroll and more), and the whole list is organized in a **Comcast/Xfinity-style category layout** with plain, people-friendly channel names: Denver Locals first (in broadcast order), then a **US Network Affiliates** section holding five Fox stations kept for football — Fox Houston (KRIV), Fox Phoenix (KSAZ), Fox Salt Lake City (KSTU), Fox Austin (KTBC) and Fox New York (WNYW), spread across time zones — then News, Sports, Movies, Entertainment, Kids & Family, Lifestyle, Documentary & Learning, and Music. Out-of-state local stations from other networks are not carried. It complements the paid service; it doesn't replace it — some premium names (HBO, Starz…) only exist in the free world as pirate relays that are dead or unreliable, so they're not carried here. The real ones stay on the paid service.

**405 channels** — locals, Fox affiliates, entertainment, sports, movies, kids, lifestyle, documentaries and music. English only. No religious channels, no Spanish-language channels, no duplicates. News is **Colorado only** (CBS News Colorado and the Denver locals), with one named exception: CNN's free streaming channels, **CNN Headlines** and **CNN Originals**. (The flagship CNN channel itself only exists in the free sources as unofficial relay streams, so it isn't carried.) **C-SPAN** is not carried either: guide data for it exists, but no free legal stream for it exists in any source checked. Dead pirate relays were removed — including five channels (beIN Sports USA, Golf Channel, Marquee Sports Network, NFL Network, AXS TV) whose only free streams turned out to be unofficial relays.

**Logos:** audited channel by channel — every logo in the list was verified to load, and dead ones were replaced with working logos from the program guides.

**Program guide (EPG):** every channel in this playlist has guide data — that's a rule, not a coincidence. Channels were matched against nine free guides (Pluto TV US & Canada, Samsung TV Plus, Plex, Rally TV, and the epgshare US / US locals / US sports guides) with several matching passes; any channel with no guide anywhere was removed. The merged guide covers **all 405 channels** (about 9,100 programs, roughly the next 24 hours) and refreshes daily. The playlist header points your player at it automatically; if your player asks for the guide separately, use:
- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gabebaca7-ctrl/Free-IPTV-5280/main/epg.xml

Three paid-mirror channels were dropped by the EPG rule because no free guide exists for them anywhere: A&E, Beach TV Panama City and Fite 24/7. They remain on the paid service. (Rally TV was on that list until its guide was found in the Rally TV guide feed — it's back, in Sports.)

One station, one guide slot: KPVM-LD (Pahrump, NV) is carried as **Movies! KPVM-LD** — its ACE Country Radio stream was swapped out, since both streams shared the station's single guide listing.
